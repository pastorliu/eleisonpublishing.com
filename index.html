WHY ELEISON

Technology in service of the text.

Eleison Publishing is built around a simple principle: specialized language work should become more efficient without becoming less careful.

We use AI where it is strongest—repetitive processing, comparison, extraction, normalization, and assisted drafting—and reserve final responsibility for human judgment. The result is a workflow designed for difficult texts, not just easy ones.
