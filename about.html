ABOUT ELEISON

Scholarship with
practical discipline.

Eleison Publishing exists to help difficult texts move from source material to reliable, usable publication without sacrificing the linguistic and editorial care they require.

OUR STANDARD

Fidelity first.

We value faithful meaning, transparent editorial decisions, consistent terminology, and respect for the character of the source. A polished result is not useful if it has quietly changed what the author said.

Our work is especially suited to historical, theological, academic, literary, and specialized texts where conventional machine translation or generic OCR can miss important distinctions.

OUR TECHNOLOGY

Modern tools.
Human responsibility.

We integrate AI and language technology into practical editorial workflows for transcription, OCR correction, alignment, comparison, terminology, drafting, and quality control.

The role of technology is to increase capacity and accuracy—not to remove responsibility. Important outputs are reviewed against the source and the requirements of the project.
