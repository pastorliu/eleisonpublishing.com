Our Mission

To restore a Lutheran mission presence for China.

Concordia Press Hankow is a project-focused nonprofit establishment in progress. Its goal is to help restore and renew the historic mission of the Lutheran Church—Missouri Synod in mainland China by making faithful Lutheran theology widely and freely available online.

Free Publications

Publishing Lutheran theological works, translations, catechetical materials, historical sources, and other resources for free online access.

Digital Mission

Using the internet to reach readers throughout China, overcoming geographical distance and making serious theological resources available wherever they can be accessed.

Focused Projects

Building practical publishing projects that recover, translate, edit, proofread, and distribute Lutheran materials for Chinese readers.

Historical Continuity

Remembering the Lutheran work in Hankow.

The name Concordia Press Hankow intentionally recalls the historic Lutheran work of the LCMS in China, including Concordia Seminary in Hankow (now Wuhan) and the publishing work associated with Concordia Press Hankow.

The Lutheran Mission in China
We seek to preserve awareness of the missionary, educational, theological, and publishing work through which Lutheran pastors and missionaries served Chinese communities.
Concordia Seminary, Hankow
The seminary represents the importance of theological education, pastoral formation, and the long-term establishment of a faithful Lutheran ministry among Chinese people.
Concordia Press Hankow
The press represents a complementary mission: placing sound Lutheran teaching into print and making it accessible to readers in their own language.
“The printed page and the digital page can both serve the same purpose: to place the treasures of the Lutheran confession within reach of those who seek them.”

A mission for the internet age.

The present project is not defined by a single institution or building. It is focused on concrete work: identifying important Lutheran texts, preparing reliable editions and translations, using modern digital tools responsibly, and distributing publications freely online to readers in China and beyond.

Recover

Locate and preserve historical Lutheran materials relevant to the mission in China.

Translate & Prepare

Make Lutheran theology accessible through careful translation, editing, OCR, proofreading, and digital preparation.

Publish & Reach

Release publications freely online and reach Chinese readers directly through the channels of the internet.
