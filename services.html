BUILT AROUND THE TEXT

Not every text needs the same workflow.

A nineteenth-century Fraktur volume, a Greek theological manuscript, a Latin critical text, and a modern Chinese translation present different problems. We begin with the source and design the process around its actual condition.

AI is used selectively for tasks where it improves speed or comparison. Final linguistic decisions remain subject to human review.
