WHY LANGUAGE SPECIALIZATION MATTERS

A text carries more than its words.

Historical and theological texts often depend on distinctions that disappear in a generic translation workflow: technical vocabulary, syntactic relationships, quotation conventions, manuscript or edition history, and the author's register.

Our language workflows therefore combine automated assistance with source comparison and human review. AI can surface patterns and accelerate repetitive work; linguistic judgment determines what the final text should say.
