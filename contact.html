PROJECT INQUIRY

Start with
the source.

A useful first message tells us what the source is, what languages are involved, its approximate size or condition, and what you want the finished work to become.

WHAT TO INCLUDE

Give us enough context to assess the work.

Source: book, manuscript, scan, PDF, bilingual file, or existing translation.

Languages: source and target languages, including historical varieties where relevant.

Condition: searchable PDF, image scan, Fraktur, handwritten material, mixed-language pages, or other complications.

Deliverable: translation, clean text, proofreading, publication-ready files, or another outcome.

Scale: approximate page count, word count, or number of volumes/files.
