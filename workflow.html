WORKFLOW

Efficient where possible.
Careful where necessary.

Our production process separates mechanical work from linguistic judgment. This makes AI useful without allowing automation to become a substitute for editorial responsibility.

01

Ingest

Collect scans, PDFs, manuscripts, bilingual files, existing translations, and project specifications.

02

Recover

OCR and normalization produce an editable textual base while preserving source structure and identifying uncertain readings.

03

Assist

AI tools support transcription, alignment, comparison, terminology extraction, consistency checks, and first-pass translation.

04

Review

Human review checks meaning, grammar, terminology, style, citations, formatting, and fidelity to the source.

05

Publish

Deliverables are structured for print, web, ebook, translation-memory systems, or archival use.

AI PRINCIPLE

Assistance, not abdication.

AI is particularly valuable for high-volume, repetitive, and comparative tasks. It can process material quickly, identify patterns, and provide useful drafts or alternatives.

But difficult language work requires judgment. Source meaning, historical context, terminology, and editorial intent are reviewed by a human rather than accepted merely because an automated output looks fluent.
